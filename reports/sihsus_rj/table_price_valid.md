# The official price table as an input, on the validation window (sihsus_rj)

Generated by `costmodel table-price --config configs/sihsus_rj.toml`. Every admission gets the SIGTAP row of its requested procedure as extra inputs (price, the stay the table assumes, points, complexity, financing). The typical cost uses the setting the search chose for it (log_l2, 255 leaves, expanding, with time) and is given without the smearing factor; the expected cost uses its own (tweedie, 63 leaves, expanding, with time). Fitted on the training window, compared on the same validation admissions; intervals from 1,000 resamples (negative = better than the first row).

## The table itself

- Tables read: 85 monthly files, 2017-12 to 2024-12.
- 95.2% of the validation admissions have a table price for their requested procedure. Added up, those prices are **36.0%** of what was paid; 22.8% of the admissions were paid within 5% of the table price. The rest of a bill is what happens during the stay (intensive care days, other procedures, materials) and complements paid on top of the table.

Before the change, these admissions had a mean table price of 1124.08 and were paid 2397.10 on average; from the change on, 1557.70 and 2686.31. The table price is +38.6%, what was paid +12.1% (not the same admissions on each side).

A gain on the month's total is only a gain if each group gets closer to what was paid. A variant that moves the re-priced procedures from too low to too high, while the others stay too low, improves the total by adding two errors of opposite sign.

Validation window only: the test window was not read and nothing was registered. Serving a model like this needs the current table at prediction time.
