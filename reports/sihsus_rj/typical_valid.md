## Typical admissions and the most expensive ones

The 10% most expensive admissions received 57.3% of everything paid in this window, and most of the error per admission falls on them. They are real bills (intensive care, major surgery, long stays), and which admission will be one of them is not known when the patient is admitted, so they are not left out: the error is read in two bands instead. The bands are cut by what was paid, known only afterwards.
